# KRMF 🎒

A tiny pixel classroom for keeping life together.

## What lives inside

- 📅 **Calendar** — see your month, week, and important little moments.
- ✏️ **Daily planner** — give every hour a home and leave notes for yourself.
- 📚 **Weekly planner** — keep the whole week open on one cozy desk.
- ✅ **To-dos** — check things off one satisfying box at a time.
- 🌱 **Habits** — grow small routines day by day.
- 🖥️ **Mac widgets** — peek at today without opening the app.
- 📱 **Web companion** — bring your plans along on your phone.
- 🌙 **Light and dark classrooms** — pick the room that feels nicest today.

Your Mac app and pocket companion stay in sync, so your plans can follow you
without losing their pixel-school charm.
